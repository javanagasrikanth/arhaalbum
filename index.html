For the family — how photos get published

Photos added from this page are published automatically — they are uploaded to the family album’s photo storage and appear for everyone, on any device, with no extra steps.

The manual steps below are only needed for items that could not be published automatically (videos, or photos whose upload failed) — those are listed under “Show publish steps for these photos” above the gallery.

  1. Put the photo or video file in the photos/ folder of the site (next to index.html).
  2. Open index.html, find the list marked MEDIA near the top of the script at the bottom, and add one line per file: 
    { src: "photos/first-steps.mp4", type: "video",
  album: "Milestones", date: "2026-11-14",
  title: "First steps in the living room" }
  3. Commit and push to GitHub — the site updates on its own in about a minute.

Albums are just names in that list — use one of the four album names below, or type a new name and it becomes a new album automatically. The month comes from the date on each line.

iPhone .heic photos: they are converted to JPEG in the browser when you add them, so the file to save into photos/ is the downloaded JPEG (with the .jpg name shown in the publish steps), not the original .heic file.

Show the album on your TV

From a computer or Android phone (Chrome): tap Cast to TV above the gallery, pick your Sony TV, and the photo you are viewing appears on the TV. Start the slideshow and each photo follows on the TV as it changes here.

From an iPhone or iPad: casting from this page is not available in iPhone browsers. Instead, mirror your whole screen: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring, and choose your Sony TV — the album shows on the TV as you browse it.

Your Sony TV needs Chromecast built-in (most Sony Android TV / Google TV models have it) for the Cast button, or AirPlay for the iPhone mirroring. The Cast button only appears when a TV is found on the same Wi-Fi.
